Sound Alert Component - Purpose & Functionality
================================================================
Project: Intelligent Sinhala Sign Language Communication Platform
Component: Sound Alert
================================================================


WHAT IS THIS COMPONENT?
---------------------------------------------------------------
The Sound Alert component is a background listening system designed
specifically for deaf and hearing-impaired users. It runs on the
user's mobile phone and continuously listens to the surrounding
environment through the phone microphone every 2.5 seconds.

When it detects a dangerous or important sound nearby, it immediately
alerts the user through the phone screen so they are aware of what
is happening around them — even if they cannot hear it.


WHAT PROBLEM DOES IT SOLVE?
---------------------------------------------------------------
Deaf and hearing-impaired people cannot hear vehicles honking at
them or emergency sirens approaching. This puts them at serious
risk in everyday situations such as:

  - Walking on the road when a car is honking to warn them
  - Not realizing an ambulance or fire truck is approaching
  - Being unaware of a police siren nearby
  - Missing warning sounds from trucks, buses, or trains

This component solves that problem by acting as the "ears" of
the application and alerting the user through visual notifications
on their phone screen.


WHAT SOUNDS DOES IT DETECT?
---------------------------------------------------------------
The system is trained to recognize the following sounds:

  Horns (vehicle warning sounds):
    - Car horn        🚗
    - Bus horn        🚌
    - Truck horn      🚛
    - Train horn      🚂

  Sirens (emergency vehicle sounds):
    - Ambulance siren 🚑
    - Fire truck siren 🚒
    - Police siren    🚓

  Note: Each of these produces its own specific alert on screen
  with a matching icon so the user knows exactly what was heard.


HOW DOES IT WORK IN REAL LIFE?
---------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The user opens the Sound Alert screen in the app and taps
     the Start Monitoring button.
  2. The phone microphone begins listening to the surroundings
     automatically every 2.5 seconds.
  3. When a horn or siren is detected with enough confidence,
     the app immediately shows a visual alert on screen.
  4. The user sees the alert and becomes aware of the danger.
  5. All detections are saved to an Alert History so the user
     can review what sounds were detected throughout the day.

Example real-life scenario:
  - A deaf person is walking near a road.
  - An ambulance is approaching from behind with its siren on.
  - The app detects the siren through the phone microphone.
  - The entire phone screen turns red and shows:
       🚑  "Ambulance Detected"
       HIGH PRIORITY
  - The phone vibrates continuously until the user taps DISMISS.
  - The user sees it, steps aside, and stays safe.


NOTIFICATION PRIORITY (FRONTEND)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Not all sounds are equally dangerous. The app uses three levels
of urgency, shown differently on screen:

  TIER 1 — EMERGENCY (Full-Screen Red Alert, must be dismissed manually):
    Triggered by the most dangerous sounds:
      - Ambulance siren  🚑
      - Fire truck siren 🚒
      - Police siren     🚓
      - Train horn       🚂  (large vehicle, high danger)
      - Truck horn       🚛  (large vehicle, high danger)
      - Bus horn         🚌  (large vehicle, high danger)

    What happens on screen:
      - The entire screen turns red
      - A large emoji and sound name appear in the centre
      - The phone vibrates continuously in a strong pattern
      - The screen flashes
      - The user must tap DISMISS to clear it

  TIER 2 — WARNING (Auto-dismiss banner, disappears after 3.5 seconds):
    Triggered by less urgent sounds:
      - Car horn   🚗
      - Motorcycle horn 🏍️

    What happens on screen:
      - A banner slides down from the top of the screen
      - Shows the sound name and a light vibration
      - Disappears automatically after a few seconds

  NO VISUAL ALERT (Silent / Ambient):
    - Background traffic noise is logged silently in history
      but does not show any banner or interrupt the user,
      because it is too frequent and not immediately dangerous.

  Priority rule:
    - If an emergency sound and a warning arrive at the same time,
      the emergency (Tier 1) alert always takes priority and
      appears on top, covering the banner completely.

  User settings:
    - The user can turn individual sound types on or off from
      the Settings screen inside the app.
    - Emergency sirens (ambulance, fire truck, police) are always
      active and cannot be turned off, for the user's safety.


SCREENS IN THE APP
---------------------------------------------------------------
  Home (Sound Monitoring):
    - Start / Stop monitoring button
    - Live list of recent alerts with time, type, and severity
    - Filter alerts by severity (All / High / Medium / Low)
    - Backend connection status indicator

  Alert History:
    - Full history of all detections grouped by Today /
      Yesterday / This Week
    - Filter by sound type or severity
    - Useful for reviewing what happened throughout the day

  Settings:
    - Toggle individual horn types on or off
    - Toggle vibration, screen flash, and banner notifications
    - Emergency sirens are always on (cannot be disabled)


WHO IS IT FOR?
---------------------------------------------------------------
  - Deaf and hearing-impaired individuals in Sri Lanka
  - People who use the Intelligent Sinhala Sign Language app
  - Anyone who needs sound-awareness assistance in daily life


WHAT DOES THE USER EXPERIENCE?
---------------------------------------------------------------
From the user's point of view, the experience is simple:

  - Open the app and press Start Monitoring
  - Go about your day normally
  - The screen reacts immediately when a dangerous sound is heard
  - Red full-screen flash = emergency nearby (ambulance / police / fire)
  - Top banner = vehicle horn nearby (car / motorcycle)
  - Check Alert History at any time to see past detections

The user does not need to understand anything about how it works.
The component does all the detection automatically and silently.


OVERALL PURPOSE
---------------------------------------------------------------
The Sound Alert component gives hearing-impaired users the ability
to be aware of dangerous sounds in their environment in real time.
It acts as a safety layer that uses the phone's microphone and
artificial intelligence to recognize threatening sounds and
immediately notify the user through clear visual alerts — helping
them stay safe in everyday situations that hearing people handle
automatically through sound.
